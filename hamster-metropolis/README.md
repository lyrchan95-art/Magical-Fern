# Hamster Metropolis: Signal Run

A single-file browser runner set in a Zootopia-style city of hamsters, where every citizen carries a Nokia-era phone. Open `index.html` in any browser. It needs no build step and no install.

## Story
The Great Dial-Up Blackout has knocked out every signal tower in the city. You play a courier hamster who runs through five districts to relight the towers before The Static spreads.

| # | District | What it adds |
|---|----------|--------------|
| 1 | Bubblegum Borough | Jumping, double jumping, sliding under low signs, platforms |
| 2 | Mint Plaza | Gaps, Weasel taxis, elephant feet (a shadow shows where each lands) |
| 3 | CRT Cove | Glitch blocks that switch on and off, rogue drones, CRT scanlines |
| 4 | Sunset Speedway | Boost pads, higher speed |
| 5 | Static Heights | The Static chases you, and every hit lets it gain ground |
| ∞ | Uncharted | Endless procedural districts with a new name and palette every 350m |

## What keeps players coming back
- **Phone HUD:** the score, a signal meter and a battery (your lives) appear on a tiny LCD. Texts from citizens pop up while you run.
- **Full Signal mode:** fill the signal meter to become invincible for a few seconds. Seeds fly to you and you smash through obstacles.
- **Combos and Clutch bonuses:** combos multiply your score up to x5. Near-misses give a CLUTCH bonus.
- **S·I·G·N·A·L letters:** collect all six in a level for 1,000 points and a free phone booth call.
- **Three stars per level:** you get one for finishing, one for finishing with at least 2 battery, and one for collecting every letter.
- **Phone booth:** spend seeds to unlock random citizens, out of 40 to collect, and choose any of them as your runner.
- **Snake bonus:** play a Snake bonus round on the phone after each level you clear to earn extra seeds.
- **Recharge:** spend 50 seeds to continue a failed run.
- **Daily Dash:** everyone gets the same course each day.
- **Share button:** copies an emoji result card to paste into a group chat.
- **Atlas:** lists every uncharted territory you've discovered.

## Controls
- Space, ↑ or W, or tap the screen: jump (press again in mid-air to double jump)
- ↓ or S, or hold the SLIDE button: slide (in mid-air, this drops you down faster)
- P or Esc: pause

Progress is saved in your browser's localStorage.
